about

MOHAMMAD ASLAM

I write slow burn romance — the kind where nothing happens for eight chapters and then something small happens and it means everything.

I'm based in India. I write in Urdu-flavoured English because that's how the conversations in my head actually sound. First language for feelings: Urdu. First language for sarcasm: also Urdu.

My first novel is out. Volume 1 is free on Wattpad, permanently. The rest will get there eventually. I'm working on it.
