About the Author

Writing the quiet spaces between big moments.

I write slow-burn fiction because life rarely changes with dramatic speeches. It changes in kitchens late at night, during awkward breakfasts, and in the small choices two people make when they decide not to walk away.

Just a Little While Apart is my first serial project. Volume 1 is completely free to read, written with care and genuine intention.
