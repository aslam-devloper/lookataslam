✦ the story

Two strangers.
One nikah. Zero manual.

💍

Ma'un

21. Overthinks everything to tribunal-level detail. Genuinely sincere at his core. Has a black watch-strap habit he never explains to anyone.

🌸

Naba

19. Speaks in short sentences. Deflects with clinical precision. The rare moments her warmth breaks through — they hit hard.

🏠

Their Story

An arranged marriage. A shared apartment. A calico kitten named Cookie. And two people slowly learning that lightness always replaces the dark.

✦ the author

MOHAMMAD ASLAM

I have a lot of stories in my head. Figured it was time to put at least one of them on paper.

I'm new to writing, so I'm still figuring things out. Just a Little While Apart is my first project — a story I genuinely care about and am writing as honestly as I can.

I write slow burns. I write characters who can't say what they mean. I write the quiet moments between the big ones, because I think that's where life actually lives.

Thanks for stopping by. ♡
