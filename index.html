About the Author

Writing quiet healing and unspoken sorrow.

I write fiction because real life rarely moves in dramatic Hollywood speeches. It moves in quiet kitchens late at night, in the hesitant courage of two strangers learning to talk to each other, and in the sudden, irreversible weight of small mistakes.

My writing covers two worlds: slow-burn contemporary romance that heals quietly, and grounded tragedies that explore grief, guilt, and the irreplaceable value of time.
