FAQ

Things people ask.

What is Just a Little While Apart about?

Two strangers who get married and have to figure out how to actually like each other. No dramatic conflict — just two awkward people trying. It's halal slice-of-life. Very human, not fantasy.

What is The Silly Mistakes of Sabu about?

A Japanese salaryman who keeps telling himself he'll fix the small things later. He never gets the chance. No villain — just the quiet weight of ordinary neglect and what it costs.

Who are you exactly?

Mohammad Aslam. I write under the name lookataslam. I'm from India. I write because I love it — halal slice-of-life stories that feel real, carry meaning, and don't shout at you about it.

Is JALWA halal content?

Yes. No explicit scenes. Romance within marriage, written carefully. It exists because halal romance that actually reads like real life — not a trope — is rare.

What's free, what's paid?

Why read this over published books?

Halal slice-of-life fiction that feels like real Muslim domestic life — not a trope checklist — is genuinely hard to find. If that's what you want, this is probably it.
