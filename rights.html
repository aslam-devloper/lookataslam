3. How We Track Distribution

Every paid copy served through this platform is individually fingerprinted at the point of delivery. This fingerprint is embedded in the content itself — it does not alter the reading experience, and it cannot be seen or removed by ordinary means.

The fingerprint is permanently linked to the transaction record of the purchase that unlocked it. If a copy of this content appears anywhere outside this platform, we can identify the source transaction from the content alone — regardless of how the content was formatted, converted, or shared.

We retain transaction records, access logs, and IP records for all purchases and reading sessions. These records are held securely and used only for the purpose of rights enforcement.

These are not idle warnings. The embedded fingerprint means attribution is not a matter of investigation — it is a matter of reading the file.
