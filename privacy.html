Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 15, 2026 · Effective Immediately

1. Overview & Our Commitment

Welcome to lookataslam (accessible at lookataslam.vercel.app), the personal author website and online reading library maintained by independent novelist Mohammad Aslam based in India.

We believe reading should be a quiet, private experience free from surveillance capitalism. We do not sell your personal data, track your browsing across the internet, or display third-party advertisements.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not track you with third-party tracking cookies or advertising pixels.
  • We do not engage in device fingerprinting or cross-site tracking.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade visitor data to brokers or advertisers.

3. Information You Voluntarily Provide

You can read all our novels without creating an account or providing any personal information. You only share information when you voluntarily choose to interact with the following features:

  • Newsletter Subscriptions: If you voluntarily enter your email to receive notifications about new chapter drops and novel releases (such as Just a Little While Apart: Volume 2), your email is securely processed via Resend strictly for delivering release updates. Every email contains a direct, one-click unsubscribe link.
  • Voluntary Reader Support / Donations: If you choose to support the author via Razorpay, payment transactions are handled directly by Razorpay (PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant). We never receive, store, or have access to your credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, net-banking credentials, or UPI PINs.
  • Reader Comments & Thoughts: If you post a public comment on a chapter, the display name and text you submit are stored to display alongside that chapter. For user safety, external links, promotional spam, phone numbers, and social media handles are automatically blocked.

4. Cookies & Local Browser Storage

We use minimal local storage (HTML5 localStorage) stored solely on your device to remember your reading preferences:

  • Your preferred reading theme (Warm, Night, or Sage).
  • Your preferred text font size in the reader.
  • Your last read chapter so you can easily resume reading where you left off.
  • Your cookie consent acknowledgement.

This data remains on your device and is never sent to ad servers. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser data or site cookies.

5. Security & Content Protection

Our website implements enterprise-grade HTTP security headers, including Content Security Policy (CSP), anti-clickjacking protection (X-Frame-Options: DENY), MIME-sniffing protection (X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff), and strict honeypot traps to prevent automated malicious abuse.

6. Contact & Your Rights

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, wish to unsubscribe, or request the deletion of your email address from our release list, please contact us directly:
